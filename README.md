## Hi, I'm Omar

I'm a development consultant with **9+ years** across mobile (native Android) and enterprise (OutSystems) development, including banking programmes and consulting work at a global systems integrator. Today I build across **OutSystems**, **native Android and iOS**, **Kotlin Multiplatform** and **Flutter**.

I'm the founder of **[Axari](https://axari.io)** (Axari OÜ), and the only person behind it. The name joins **Ax** (balance) and **Ari** (harmony), and that is how I try to run every engagement. I work directly with teams and as a subcontractor for consulting agencies serving clients across the EU.

## What I do

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **OutSystems** | O11 and ODC applications: reactive web and mobile, integrations, code review, and delivery inside larger programmes. OutSystems certified. |
| **Native mobile** | Kotlin on Android and Swift on iOS when the product needs platform performance, store readiness, and a clear path to release. |
| **Kotlin Multiplatform** | One shared codebase across mobile, desktop, Wasm web, and server. Less duplication, faster iteration. |

## How I work

- **Scope before code.** Requirements, constraints, and success criteria are locked before the first commit. You know what will ship, and what will not.
- **EU remote delivery.** Remote across the Union, in English, with Italian and Slovene when the engagement needs them.
- **Ownership.** New products, single flows, or enterprise work already in flight: I join your cadence and take ownership of the outcome.
- **AI under control.** AI accelerates delivery. Architecture, risk, and release quality stay with a senior engineer, never with the model alone.
